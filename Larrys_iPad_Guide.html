Larry's iPad Guide

Tap a topic.

Home Screen

  • Swipe up from the bottom without pausing.
  • Pause while swiping opens the App Switcher.

Dock

  • Left side: your permanent apps and folders.
  • Right side: recent/suggested apps.

Folders

  1. Touch and hold a blank area until icons jiggle.
  2. Drag one app onto another.
  3. Pause, then release.

Screenshots

Larry's iPad Guide

Home Screen

  1. Tap an app once to open it.
  2. Touch and hold an app to move it.
  3. Drag one app onto another to make a folder.
  4. Tap Done when finished.

Dock

  1. Swipe up slightly from the bottom edge and stop.
  2. Tap an app in the Dock to open it.
  3. Touch and hold an app and drag it into the Dock to add it.

Files App

  1. Open Files.
  2. Tap Browse.
  3. Tap Downloads.
  4. Touch and hold a file to rename, move, share, or delete it.

Safari

  1. Tap the address bar.
  2. Type a website address or search words.
  3. Tap Go.
  4. Use the tab button to open or close tabs.
  5. Use Share to add a bookmark.

Copy and Paste

  1. Touch and hold the text.
  2. Drag the blue handles.
  3. Tap Copy.
  4. Touch and hold at the destination.
  5. Tap Paste.

Use Two Apps at Once

  1. Open the first app.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top.
  3. Tap Split View.
  4. Select the second app.
  5. Drag the center divider to resize.

Take a Screenshot

  1. Without a Home button: press the top button and either volume button together.
  2. With a Home button: press the top button and Home button together.
  3. Release immediately.
Screenshots are saved in Photos.

Wi-Fi

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Tap Wi-Fi.
  3. Tap your network name.
  4. Enter the password.
  5. Tap Join.

Install and Update Apps

  1. Open the App Store.
  2. Tap Search.
  3. Find the app.
  4. Tap Get.
  5. For updates, tap your profile picture, then Update All.

Restart a Frozen iPad

  1. Press and release the volume button nearest the top button.
  2. Press and release the other volume button.
  3. Hold the top button until the Apple logo appears.
